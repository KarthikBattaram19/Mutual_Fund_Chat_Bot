Explain the tax implications of long-term capital gains in equity mutual funds.

sync Analyzing scheme data...
verified VERIFIED RESPONSE

For equity mutual funds, Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) apply if you hold the units for more than 1 year. Currently, LTCG up to ₹1.25 Lakhs per financial year is exempt from tax. Any gains exceeding this threshold are taxed at a flat rate of 12.5% without indexation benefits.

Source: IT Act Sec 112A
